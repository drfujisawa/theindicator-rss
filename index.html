The Indicator — Full History RSS Feed

This is an unofficial archival RSS feed for NPR's The Indicator from Planet Money.

NPR's official RSS feed only keeps a limited number of recent episodes. This feed preserves episodes instead of removing them as they disappear from NPR's official feed.

How it works

The archive automatically checks NPR's official feed for new episodes. New episodes are added to this feed, while previously archived episodes are retained.

The next goal is to restore older episodes dating back to the launch of The Indicator in 2017.
