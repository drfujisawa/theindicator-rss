The Indicator — Full History RSS Feed

This is an unofficial archival RSS feed for NPR's The Indicator from Planet Money. The archive covers 1,760+ episodes from April 2018 to the present.

NPR's official RSS feed only keeps a limited number of recent episodes. This feed preserves every episode instead of removing them as they disappear from NPR's official feed. A small number of very early episodes could not be matched to a still-accessible audio file and are excluded.

How it works

A GitHub Actions workflow checks NPR's official feed every six hours. New episodes are added to the archive automatically while all previously archived episodes are retained permanently.

Historical episodes back to April 2018 were recovered by crawling NPR's web archive and resolving audio URLs from NPR's CDN and affiliate sources.
